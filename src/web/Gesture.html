Gesture Control

Click the button below to start or stop gesture recognition.

Available Gestures

✌️ V Gesture: Move cursor

👊 Fist: Click and drag

🖕 Middle finger: Left click

☝️ Index finger: Right click

🤏 Pinch (major hand): Adjust brightness/volume

🤏 Pinch (minor hand): Scroll horizontally/vertically

✌️ (closed): Double click
